In short

rtexjp is an engine that assembles short vertical illustrated videos (reels) in navy blue from a script written in a text file (roteiro.json). It is the engine Nei uses for INEMA reels, open to anyone who wants to study or adapt it: no need to record yourself or pay for an avatar service. It generates the voice, draws each scene frame by frame, mixes the soundtrack, and checks on its own that the video follows the format. You need a computer with a graphics card (GPU), Python, Node, ffmpeg, and two local servers: one for voice (inemavox) and one for images (flux2-klein).

It comes configured with Nei’s voice and avatar. To use your own, point the environment variables RTEXJP_VOZ_REF (voice reference recording) and RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF (avatar photo) to your files, describe your character in RTEXJP_AVATAR_DESC (otherwise the poses will have Nei’s cap, glasses, and beard), and generate the poses again; you don’t need to edit any code. These two files are not included in the repository (see Prerequisites).

What it is

A fixed format checked by a validator

Every reel follows the same structure: a hook in frame 0, proof with a source, the viewer’s problem, numbered points, an honest caveat, a maxim, and an opinion question. The engine draws it; the validator flags anything that falls outside the format.

🎨 Illustrated, no lip sync

A large illustrated avatar of Nei at the bottom (≈ 43 % of the height), in 11 poses tied to the emotion of the speech (startled, unsure, pointing, explaining, thumbs-up…), one hero illustration per scene, and a robot mascot. No one needs to record anything.

🎙️ Nei’s voice, local

Voice generated with local TTS (inemavox chatterbox), at 160–190 words per minute. Local Whisper marks the timing of each word for captions and scene triggers.

✅ Automatic QA

Measures duration, speech pace, volume (−14 LUFS), visual changes per minute, proof within 20 s, options planted before the prompt, CTA without a delivery promise, final card, and silence before the maxim. It produces a qa.md and a contact.jpg.

The format

The nine rules of the rtexjp standard

These are the rules enforced by the checar command and QA for every edit.

  1. A hook in frame 0, of one of these types: contrary (tells people to stop doing something everyone does), loss (what if X disappeared?), fear statistic with a source, or a third-party story with a number. Not allowed: lists ("5 things…"), "did you know…", "I did…".
  2. Frame 0 headline of up to ~6 words, in uppercase, with one word in yellow, more forceful than the speech. Draw an object with visible damage (scratched in red, broken); the avatar reacts in the first second, and something changes every ~1 s in the first three seconds. Frame 0 serves as the cover.
  3. Proof within 20 s: a real screenshot of the source page, the number in the headline, and the fonte: line below.
  4. The viewer’s problem, with reassurance ("it’s not your fault; no one gave you the map"). The final question is planted here: the options appear in yellow.
  5. One topic per video, in 2–5 points with a visible index and a pill bar at the top; one object per sentence, changing every 2–3 s.
  1. Every number has a written source on screen (fonte: …).
  2. An honest caveat, spoken aloud, on an alert card (red = problem, green = correct).
  3. A maxim in antithesis, after 0.8 s of real silence (voice and soundtrack stop).
  4. An opinion CTA: Nei states his side and asks a strong question that can be answered with one word ("comment DONO or REFÉM"). The card shows the question and options; the comment box types one option, posts it, and confirms it. It stays for ~10 s, with no sign-off. Never promise to deliver anything ("I’ll send it to you," "I’ll send you"): no one replies to comments. The link and source stay in the pinned comment.

Quick glossary

Opinion prompt

The question at the end, with two one-word options (in the 2nd video, DONO or REFÉM). The tension appears in frame 0, the options are planted early, and only those (and the headline’s hot word) are highlighted in yellow.

Maxim

The summary line at the end, in antithesis ("Being the owner of your system was never about knowing every line. It’s about knowing what holds what in place."). It comes after a short silence.

Stage

The motor/palco.html page that draws each scene. The renderer takes a snapshot of it for every frame.

Music bed

Quiet music under the voice that gets quieter when the voice speaks and stops during the silence before the maxim.

LUFS

A measure of perceived loudness. −14 LUFS is the volume used as a standard by social platforms.

CTA

"Call to action": the video’s final request. Here it is always a single opinion question answered with one word.

TTS and lip sync

TTS is a voice generated from text. Lip sync is mouth movement matching the speech; rtexjp does not do that, the avatar only changes poses.

Whisper and Playwright

Whisper transcribes the voice and times each word. Playwright opens the stage in a headless browser and takes a snapshot of each frame.

Typography flash

A quick screen with only large text between points, so the video doesn’t feel static.

Block types

tese = claim (the hook, the problem); autoridade = proof with a source; virada = the index that introduces the points; metodo = each point; planta is just the id of the block that plants the options for the final question.

Beat

A section of a block that changes the headline, illustration, and pose on the word marked in em. It makes something change every 2–3 s.

Hero illustration

The large illustrated object in the middle of the screen (broken terminal, map, padlock), taken from the motor/assets/objetos/ library.

The v1.2 look, from top to bottom

  1. Label in monospace (≥ 30 px) and, for the points, pills 01–03.
  2. Headline in Sora ExtraBold, centered, 2–3 lines, ≥ 96 px, almost white, with one word in light steel blue (yellow only in frame 0).
  3. Hero illustration or real screenshot, large, between the headline and avatar.
  4. Karaoke captions in their own strip, with the spoken word highlighted.
  5. Large avatar, at the bottom and centered, in the beat’s pose; the face stays above 80 % of the frame height.

The background has a spotlight and light grain and alternates between navy and ice-blue paper ("tema": "claro" in the scene), with navy text. During the silence before the maxim, the last frame freezes with the avatar looking thoughtful: the screen is never blank.

Visual quality gate: in two samples per block, QA measures the headline (≥ 96 px), all text (≥ 30 px), occupied area (≥ 55 % of the usable frame), text contrast (≥ 4.5:1), and avatar height (≥ 38 %). Applies to scripts with "versao": "rtexjp/3".

How it works

Six steps, one command each

rtexjp.py runs the steps in order. The stage is an HTML page drawn by window.render(t): the same build/cena.json always produces the same video. The voice does not: TTS comes out a little differently each time (so the voz step lets you redo just a few blocks).

roteiro.json→ checar→ voice + Whisper→ cena→ render (Playwright, 30 fps)→ mix (−14 LUFS)→ qa→ final.mp4
1

Script and validation

checar checks the structure using the tipo field: it starts with tese, has a virada before the first metodo (2–5 points), a ressalva before the maxima, and the maxima immediately before the final cta. The 2nd example uses tese (hook) → autoridade (proof) → tese (problem + options) → virada → metodo ×3 → ressalva → maxima → cta.

2

Voice and scene

voz generates one audio file per block, speeds it up to 1.15×, and joins them; cena combines the script with the timing of each word.

3

Render, mix, and QA

render snapshots the stage frame by frame; mix adds the soundtrack (which stops during the maxim’s silence); qa measures everything.

Prerequisites

What needs to be on your machine

Everything runs locally. No paid APIs are called. Paths come from environment variables, with Nei’s machine as the default; if your setup is different, export the variables (table below) and you don’t need to change any code.

Voice: inemavox + chatterbox environment

Clone the inematds/inemavox project to ~/projetos/inemavox (the engine calls its tts_direct.py and whisper_daemon.py). Chatterbox TTS and Whisper large-v3 run in a separate Python environment (on Nei’s machine, the conda env chatterbox at ~/miniconda3/envs/chatterbox); if yours is elsewhere, point the variables below to it. If you clone it somewhere else, export RTEXJP_INEMAVOX with the path. Whisper large-v3 uses about 10 GB of GPU memory (NVIDIA), plus chatterbox. Tested on Linux.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/inemavox ~/projetos/inemavox
cd ~/projetos/inemavox && python3 -m venv venv
./venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt   # o rtexjp chama venv/bin/python tts_direct.py
# chatterbox + faster-whisper num ambiente próprio (ver o README do inemavox)
export CHATTERBOX_PYTHON=/caminho/do/env/bin/python3   # TTS
export CS_WHISPER_PY=/caminho/do/env/bin/python3       # Whisper

Image: flux2-klein

The inematds/inemaimg server with the flux2-klein model (default http://localhost:8000/generate, or the address in RTEXJP_IMG_URL), plus the avatar reference photo (RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF). Needed only for optional steps 4 and 5 (poses and scene illustrations); Nei’s poses and the illustration library are already included.

Node, ffmpeg, Python

Node 18 or newer, ffmpeg (includes ffprobe), and Python 3 with Pillow and numpy.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/rtexjp
cd rtexjp
npm i
npx playwright install chromium
pip install --user pillow numpy   # ou dentro de um venv

User guide · step by step

From script to final.mp4

Each edit lives in edicoes/<id>/. The tudo shortcut (step 10) runs checar, voice, scene, render, mix, and QA in one go (without the frame preview from step 7).

1

Set your voice reference you

The voice step clones the voice from a short recording. The default points to Nei’s recording, which is not included in the repository; without setting your own, the voz step fails. Record 10 to 20 seconds of clear speech in WAV format and export RTEXJP_VOZ_REF. If you are also changing the avatar, export RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF with a photo of yourself and RTEXJP_AVATAR_DESC with your features and clothing, then run step 4.

export RTEXJP_VOZ_REF=~/minha-voz.wav
export RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF=~/minha-foto.jpg   # só se for trocar o avatar
2

Write the script you

Copy the example script and replace its content. The format is summarized just below the guide, in "The roteiro.json file".

mkdir -p edicoes/minha-edicao
cp edicoes/2026-10-28-mapa/roteiro.json edicoes/minha-edicao/   # depois edite
3

Validate the format rules

No taste judgments, just rules. It rejects: incorrect block order; fewer than 2 or more than 5 points; an index that doesn’t match the number of points; digits or exclamation marks in speech (spell out numbers); a fonte that does not exist in fontes; a word in destaque_fala or a trigger that is not in the speech; a number on screen without a source; silence before the maxim outside 0.5–0.9 s. Hook: gancho.tipo not in the list; list/"did you know"/"I did" hooks; a frame 0 headline with more than ~6 words, no uppercase, or no hot word; no damaged object; proof after the 3rd block. Prompt: question without "?"; options that are not a single uppercase word; an option missing from the CTA speech or not planted earlier; Nei’s side not stated before the question; a deliverable; or a CTA promising delivery ("I’ll send it to you," "I’ll send you," "comment X and…"). Scripts in the old format (keyword + deliverable, without a hook) pass with a LEGACY warning only.

python3 rtexjp.py checar edicoes/minha-edicao
4

Avatar poses optional

Nei’s 11 poses (steel blue T-shirt) are already in motor/avatar/: susto, surpreso, duvida, queixo, apontando, olha, explica, polegar, bracos, segurando, mostrando. Run this only if you changed the character: the photo (RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF) and the description of features and clothing (RTEXJP_AVATAR_DESC, in English, included in the flux2-klein prompt). Pass pose names to regenerate only those. Check each pose before continuing: the green background is removed automatically, but a distorted hand or dark background means you should try again.

python3 motor/gerar_avatar.py              # todas
python3 motor/gerar_avatar.py susto duvida  # só essas
5

Hero illustrations and proof screenshot optional

Each beat in the script shows a large illustrated object in the same flat style, between the headline and avatar. They live in a reusable library, motor/assets/objetos/<nome>.png, with the prompt and seed in indice.json. Ten are already included (broken terminal, map with pins, torn map, magnifying glass over files, explanation bubble, dominoes, cost alert, folder, key, padlock). To create a new one, describe the object in English; if it comes out distorted or blotchy, change the seed. Proof uses a real screenshot of the source page, stored in edicoes/<id>/prova/ and referenced in the beat’s print field.

python3 motor/gerar_objeto.py foguete "a small blue rocket taking off" 11
python3 motor/gerar_objeto.py --lista
6

Voice for each block + timing for each word

One audio file per block with Nei’s voice; Whisper marks each word in build/tempos.json. Pass block names to redo only those. Whisper runs as the inemavox server (whisper_daemon.py, using the Python from CS_WHISPER_PY); if it does not start, the engine uses inemavox’s transcrever_v1.py with the same Python. The log is in ~/.cache/whisper_daemon.log.

python3 rtexjp.py voz edicoes/minha-edicao            # todos os blocos
python3 rtexjp.py voz edicoes/minha-edicao p2 ressalva  # só esses
7

Build the scene and check individual frames

cena writes build/cena.json. Before the full render, take a few frames (in seconds) to review.

python3 rtexjp.py cena edicoes/minha-edicao
node motor/render.mjs edicoes/minha-edicao/build --quadros 0,10,20   # -> edicoes/minha-edicao/quadros/
8

Render at 1080×1920 and 30 fps

Playwright snapshots the stage frame by frame, and ffmpeg combines them into build/video.mp4 (without sound).

python3 rtexjp.py render edicoes/minha-edicao
9

Mix and QA

Voice + quiet soundtrack (which stops during the maxim’s silence), with final volume at −14 LUFS. The track in motor/assets/trilha.mp3 is the music bed for Nei’s reels: to publish your own, point RTEXJP_TRILHA to music with a license you know; then QA writes qa.md, build/qa.json, and contact.jpg. Any failed criterion appears with ❌ in qa.md, and the command ends with a Python error (CalledProcessError): that means QA failed, it is not a bug.

python3 rtexjp.py mix edicoes/minha-edicao   # -> final.mp4
python3 rtexjp.py qa  edicoes/minha-edicao   # -> qa.md, contact.jpg
10

Or run everything at once

Runs checar → voice → scene → render → mix → QA. Stops if checar fails.

python3 rtexjp.py tudo edicoes/minha-edicao
11

Review before publishing you

Open contact.jpg and watch the video. The title, description (ending with the question), and pinned comment (with the repository link and source, simply listed, not offered as a reward) are in the script’s publicar block. Publishing is outside this repository.

The roteiro.json file

The script format at a glance

An object with id, versao, titulo, gancho, isca, fontes, the blocos list, and the publicar block. A shortened excerpt from the real 2nd example (// lines are not valid JSON; the full file is in edicoes/2026-10-28-mapa/roteiro.json):

{
  "id": "2026-10-28-mapa", "versao": "rtexjp/2",
  "gancho": { "tipo": "perda", "objeto": "terminal do Claude Code rachado em vermelho..." },
  "isca": { "tipo": "opiniao", "pergunta": "Você é dono ou refém do sistema que a IA montou?",
            "opcoes": ["DONO", "REFÉM"], "lado": "Eu quero ser dono do meu" },
  "fontes": [ { "id": "ua", "valor": "88,7 mil estrelas", "linha": "github.com/Egonex-AI/Understand-Anything · 28/10/2026" } ],
  "blocos": [
    { "id": "tese", "tipo": "tese", "pose": "surpreso",
      "fala": "Se o Claude Code sumisse amanhã, você saberia mexer no sistema que ele montou pra você?",
      "fala_tts": "Se o Clôud Côud sumisse amanhã, ...",
      "tela": { "texto": "O CLAUDE SUMIU. E AGORA?", "destaque": "SUMIU" },
      "cena": { "comp": "gancho", "params": { "carimbo": "FORA DO AR", ... } } },
    // ... autoridade (prova), tese (problema + opções), virada, metodo ×3, ressalva ...
    { "id": "maxima", "tipo": "maxima", "silencio_antes_s": 0.8, "cena": { "comp": "flash" },
      "fala": "Ser dono do teu sistema nunca foi saber cada linha. É saber o que segura o quê." },
    { "id": "cta", "tipo": "cta", "hold_s": 7.5, "cena": { "comp": "opiniao", "params": { "digita": "DONO" } },
      "fala": "Eu quero ser dono do meu. E você, hoje: dono ou refém do sistema que a IA montou? Comenta DONO ou REFÉM." }
  ],
  "publicar": { "titulo": "...", "descricao": "... termina na pergunta?", "comentario_fixado": "link do repo + fonte" }
}

Fields in each block

tipo (order above) · fala (spelled out, no digits) · fala_tts (optional, spelling to help TTS pronounce it correctly) · tela (large text and highlighted word) · cena.comp + cena.params (what the stage draws; params.gatilhos ties a scene change to a word in the speech) · avatar pose · destaque_fala (colored caption words) · fonte (id in fontes) · rotulo and n (point number). Only in maxima: silencio_antes_s (0.5–0.9). Only in cta: hold_s, how long the card stays after the last word (5–9 s; with the speech, the whole card lasts ~10 s). At the top level: gancho.tipo (contrario, perda, estatistica, historia) and gancho.objeto; isca with tipo: "opiniao", pergunta, opcoes, and lado.

Stage components (cena.comp)

Current format: gancho (frame 0 with damaged object), contador (supports decimais), duas (problem + prompt options), indice, mapa, explica, impacto, ressalva, flash, opiniao (question card). From the 1st edition, still on the stage: titulo, frase, pasta, repo, carro, equipe, cta (legacy). The robot mascot is drawn by the stage itself, beside the avatar, and reacts to the scene (question mark, alert, check mark). Poses: bracos, queixo, apontando, segurando, surpreso, mostrando. Each component’s parameters are in the examples edicoes/2026-10-28-mapa/roteiro.json and edicoes/2026-10-28-5-coisas/roteiro.json.
