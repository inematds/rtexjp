In short

rtexjp is an engine that assembles short illustrated vertical videos (reels) in navy blue from a script written in a text file (roteiro.json). It's the engine Nei uses for INEMA reels, open to anyone who wants to study or adapt it: no need to film yourself or pay for an avatar service. It generates the voice, draws each scene frame by frame, mixes in the soundtrack, and checks that the video follows the format. It requires a computer with a graphics card (GPU), Python, Node, ffmpeg, and two local servers: one for voice (inemavox) and one for images (flux2-klein).

It comes configured with Nei's voice and avatar. To use your own, point the RTEXJP_VOZ_REF environment variable (voice reference recording) and RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF (avatar photo) to your files and regenerate the poses; no code edits are needed. These two files are not included in the repository (see Prerequisites).

What it is

A fixed format checked by a validator

Every reel follows the same structure: a hook in frame 0, evidence with a source, the viewer's problem, numbered pieces, an honest caveat, a maxim, and an opinion question. The engine draws it; the validator flags anything that falls outside the format.

🎨 Illustrated, no lip-sync

Nei's illustrated avatar in six poses (arms crossed, hand on chin, pointing, holding, surprised, showing) and a robot mascot. No one needs to record anything.

🎙️ Nei's local voice

Voice generated locally with TTS (inemavox chatterbox), at 160–190 words per minute, with local Whisper timestamping every word for captions and scene triggers.

✅ Automatic QA

Measures duration, speech pace, volume (−14 LUFS), visual changes per minute, evidence by 20 s, options planted before the CTA, a CTA with no delivery promise, the final card, and silence before the maxim. Produces a qa.md and a contact.jpg.

The format

The nine rules of the rtexjp standard

These are the checks enforced by the checar command and QA for every edit.

  1. Hook in frame 0, of one of these types: contrarian (tells viewers to stop doing something everyone does), loss (what if X disappeared?), fear statistic with a source, or a third-party story with a number. Forbidden: lists ("5 things…"), "did you know…", "I did…".
  2. Headline in frame 0 with about 6 words max, uppercase, one word in yellow, more aggressive than the narration. A damaged object is drawn (crossed out in red, broken), the avatar reacts in the first second, and something changes every ~1 s during the first three seconds. Frame 0 serves as the cover.
  3. Evidence by 20 s: a counter lands on the number and the source appears below.
  4. The viewer's problem, with absolution ("Não é culpa sua. Ninguém te deu o mapa." [It's not your fault. No one gave you the map.]). The final question is planted here: the options appear in yellow.
  5. One topic per video, in 2–5 pieces with a visible index and a pill bar at the top; one object per sentence, changing every 2–3 s.
  1. Every number has a written source on screen (fonte: …).
  2. An honest spoken caveat, in an alert card (red = problem, green = correct).
  3. A maxim in antithesis, after 0.8 s of actual silence (voice and soundtrack stop).
  4. Opinion CTA: Nei states his side and asks a strong question, answered with one word ("comenta DONO ou REFÉM" [comment DONO (OWNER) or REFÉM (HOSTAGE)]). The card shows the question and options; the comment box types an option, posts it, and confirms it. It stays for ~10 s, with no sign-off. Never promise delivery ("I'll send it to you"): no one replies to comments. The link and source stay pinned in the comment.

Quick glossary

Opinion prompt

The closing question, with two one-word options (in the second video, DONO or REFÉM). The tension appears in frame 0, the options are planted early, and only those (plus the headline's hot word) appear in yellow.

Maxim

The closing summary sentence, in antithesis ("Ser dono do teu sistema nunca foi saber cada linha. É saber o que segura o quê." [Owning your system has never meant knowing every line. It means knowing what holds what together.]). It comes after a short silence.

Stage

The motor/palco.html page that draws each scene. The renderer takes a snapshot of it for every frame.

Music bed

Low soundtrack under the voice; it dips while the voice speaks and stops during the silence before the maxim.

LUFS

A measure of perceived loudness. −14 LUFS is the loudness level social platforms use as a standard.

CTA

"Call to action": the video's final request. Here it's always just one thing: an opinion question answered with one word.

TTS and lip-sync

TTS is a voice generated from text. Lip-sync is when the mouth moves along with the speech; rtexjp doesn't do that. The avatar only changes poses.

Whisper and Playwright

Whisper transcribes the voice and timestamps every word. Playwright opens the stage in a headless browser and takes a snapshot of every frame.

Typography flash

A quick screen with large text between pieces, so the video doesn't feel static.

How it works

Six steps, one command each

rtexjp.py runs the steps in order. The stage is an HTML page drawn by window.render(t): the same build/cena.json always produces the same video. The voice doesn't: TTS comes out a little differently each time (that's why the voz step can redo just a few blocks).

roteiro.json→ checar→ voz + Whisper→ cena→ render (Playwright, 30 fps)→ mix (−14 LUFS)→ qa→ final.mp4
1

Script and validation

checar checks the structure using the tipo field: starts with tese, virada before the first metodo (2–5 pieces), ressalva before maxima, and maxima right before cta at the end. The second example uses tese (hook) → autoridade (evidence) → tese (problem + options) → virada → metodo ×3 → ressalva → maxima → cta.

2

Voice and scene

voz generates one audio file per block, speeds it up to 1.15×, and joins them; cena combines the script with each word's timing.

3

Render, mix, and QA

render captures the stage frame by frame; mix adds the soundtrack (which stops during the maxim's silence); qa measures everything.

Prerequisites

What you need on your machine

Everything runs locally. No paid APIs are called. Paths come from environment variables, with Nei's machine defaults; if yours is different, export the variables (table below) and you won't need to change any code.

Voice: inemavox + chatterbox environment

The inematds/inemavox project cloned to ~/projetos/inemavox (the engine calls its tts_direct.py and whisper_daemon.py). Chatterbox TTS and Whisper large-v3 run in a separate Python environment (Nei uses the conda env chatterbox at ~/miniconda3/envs/chatterbox); if yours is elsewhere, point the variables below to it. If you clone it elsewhere, export RTEXJP_INEMAVOX with the path. Whisper large-v3 uses about 10 GB of GPU memory (NVIDIA GPU), plus chatterbox. Tested on Linux.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/inemavox ~/projetos/inemavox
cd ~/projetos/inemavox && python3 -m venv venv
./venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt   # rtexjp calls venv/bin/python tts_direct.py
# chatterbox + faster-whisper in its own environment (see the inemavox README)
export CHATTERBOX_PYTHON=/caminho/do/env/bin/python3   # TTS
export CS_WHISPER_PY=/caminho/do/env/bin/python3       # Whisper

Images: flux2-klein

The inematds/inemaimg server with the flux2-klein model (default http://localhost:8000/generate, or the address in RTEXJP_IMG_URL), plus the avatar reference photo (RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF). Only needed for optional steps 4 and 5 (poses and scene images); Nei's six poses are already included.

Node, ffmpeg, Python

Node 18 or newer, ffmpeg (with ffprobe, which comes with it), and Python 3 with Pillow and numpy.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/rtexjp
cd rtexjp
npm i
npx playwright install chromium
pip install --user pillow numpy   # or in a venv

User guide · step by step

From script to final.mp4

Each edit lives in edicoes/<id>/. The tudo shortcut (step 10) runs checar, voz, cena, render, mix, and qa in one go (without the frame previews from step 7).

1

Set your voice reference you

The voice step clones the voice from a short recording. The default points to Nei's recording, which isn't included in the repository; the voz step will fail unless you point it to your own. Record about 10 to 20 seconds of clean speech in WAV format, and export RTEXJP_VOZ_REF. If you're also changing the avatar, export RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF with a photo of yourself and run step 4.

export RTEXJP_VOZ_REF=~/minha-voz.wav
export RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF=~/minha-foto.jpg   # only if changing the avatar
2

Write the script you

Copy the example script and replace its content. The format is summarized just below the guide in "The roteiro.json file".

mkdir -p edicoes/minha-edicao
cp edicoes/2026-10-28-mapa/roteiro.json edicoes/minha-edicao/   # edit it afterwards
3

Validate the format rules

No subjectivity, just rules. It rejects: blocks in the wrong order, fewer than 2 or more than 5 pieces, an index that doesn't match the number of pieces, digits or exclamation marks in the narration (spell out numbers), a fonte that doesn't exist in fontes, a destaque_fala or trigger word that isn't in the narration, an on-screen number without a source, or silence before the maxim outside the 0.5–0.9 s range. Hook checks: gancho.tipo not on the list, a list/"did you know"/"I did" hook, a frame 0 headline with more than about 6 words, no uppercase or no hot word, no damaged object, or evidence after the third block. Opinion prompt checks: a question without "?", options that aren't one uppercase word, an option missing from the CTA narration or not planted earlier, Nei's side not stated before the question, a deliverable, or a CTA promising delivery ("I'll send it", "comment X and…"). Older-format scripts (word + deliverable, no hook) pass with a LEGACY warning only.

python3 rtexjp.py checar edicoes/minha-edicao
4

Avatar poses optional

Nei's six poses (steel-blue T-shirt) are already in motor/avatar/. Run this only if you changed the reference photo (RTEXJP_AVATAR_REF) or the character's clothes.

python3 motor/gerar_avatar.py
5

Scene images optional

One image from local flux2-klein (default size 1152×784). Currently, only the stage's carro component uses an image (motor/assets/carro.png); a new image needs a component that loads it in motor/palco.html.

python3 motor/gerar_imagem.py motor/assets/x.png "<prompt>"
6

Voice for each block + word timestamps

One audio file per block with Nei's voice; Whisper timestamps each word in build/tempos.json. Pass block names to redo only those. If Whisper fails, the log is at ~/.cache/whisper_daemon.log.

python3 rtexjp.py voz edicoes/minha-edicao            # all blocks
python3 rtexjp.py voz edicoes/minha-edicao p2 ressalva  # only these
7

Build the scene and check individual frames

cena writes build/cena.json. Before the full render, capture a few frames (in seconds) to inspect.

python3 rtexjp.py cena edicoes/minha-edicao
node motor/render.mjs edicoes/minha-edicao/build --quadros 0,10,20   # -> edicoes/minha-edicao/quadros/
8

Render at 1080×1920 and 30 fps

Playwright captures the stage frame by frame and ffmpeg combines them into build/video.mp4 (without sound).

python3 rtexjp.py render edicoes/minha-edicao
9

Mixing and QA

Voice + low soundtrack (which stops during the maxim's silence), final loudness at −14 LUFS; then QA writes qa.md, build/qa.json, and contact.jpg. Any failed criterion appears with ❌ in qa.md, and the command ends with a Python error (CalledProcessError): that's a failed QA check, not a bug.

python3 rtexjp.py mix edicoes/minha-edicao   # -> final.mp4
python3 rtexjp.py qa  edicoes/minha-edicao   # -> qa.md, contact.jpg
10

Or run everything at once

Runs checar → voz → cena → render → mix → qa. Stops if checar fails.

python3 rtexjp.py tudo edicoes/minha-edicao
11

Review before publishing you

Open contact.jpg and watch the video. The title, description (ending with the question), and pinned comment (with the repository link and source, as fixed references rather than a reward) are in the script's publicar block. Publishing takes place outside this repository.

The roteiro.json file

The script format, at a glance

An object with id, versao, titulo, gancho, isca, fontes, the blocos list, and the publicar block. A shortened excerpt from the real second example (the // lines aren't valid JSON; the full file is at edicoes/2026-10-28-mapa/roteiro.json):

{
  "id": "2026-10-28-mapa", "versao": "rtexjp/2",
  "gancho": { "tipo": "perda", "objeto": "terminal do Claude Code rachado em vermelho..." },
  "isca": { "tipo": "opiniao", "pergunta": "Você é dono ou refém do sistema que a IA montou?",
            "opcoes": ["DONO", "REFÉM"], "lado": "Eu quero ser dono do meu" },
  "fontes": [ { "id": "ua", "valor": "88,7 mil estrelas", "linha": "github.com/Egonex-AI/Understand-Anything · 28/10/2026" } ],
  "blocos": [
    { "id": "tese", "tipo": "tese", "pose": "surpreso",
      "fala": "Se o Claude Code sumisse amanhã, você saberia mexer no sistema que ele montou pra você?",
      "fala_tts": "Se o Clôud Côud sumisse amanhã, ...",
      "tela": { "texto": "O CLAUDE SUMIU. E AGORA?", "destaque": "SUMIU" },
      "cena": { "comp": "gancho", "params": { "carimbo": "FORA DO AR", ... } } },
    // ... autoridade (prova), tese (problema + opções), virada, metodo ×3, ressalva ...
    { "id": "maxima", "tipo": "maxima", "silencio_antes_s": 0.8, "cena": { "comp": "flash" },
      "fala": "Ser dono do teu sistema nunca foi saber cada linha. É saber o que segura o quê." },
    { "id": "cta", "tipo": "cta", "hold_s": 7.5, "cena": { "comp": "opiniao", "params": { "digita": "DONO" } },
      "fala": "Eu quero ser dono do meu. E você, hoje: dono ou refém do sistema que a IA montou? Comenta DONO ou REFÉM." }
  ],
  "publicar": { "titulo": "...", "descricao": "... termina na pergunta?", "comentario_fixado": "link do repo + fonte" }
}

Fields in each block

tipo (order above) · fala (spell out numbers, no digits) · fala_tts (optional, pronunciation-friendly spelling for TTS) · tela (large text and highlighted word) · cena.comp + cena.params (what the stage draws; params.gatilhos ties a scene change to a word in the narration) · avatar pose · destaque_fala (colored words in captions) · fonte (id in fontes) · rotulo and n (piece number). Only in maxima: silencio_antes_s (0.5–0.9). Only in cta: hold_s, how long the card stays after the last word (5–9 s; with the narration, the whole card lasts about 10 s). At the top level: gancho.tipo (contrario, perda, estatistica, historia) and gancho.objeto; isca with tipo: "opiniao", pergunta, opcoes, and lado.

Stage components (cena.comp)

Current format: gancho (frame 0 with a damaged object), contador (supports decimais), duas (problem + opinion prompt options), indice, mapa, explica, impacto, ressalva, flash, opiniao (question card). From the first edition, still on the stage: titulo, frase, pasta, repo, carro, equipe, cta (legacy). The robot mascot is drawn by the stage itself, beside the avatar, and reacts to the scene (question mark, alert, check mark). Poses: bracos, queixo, apontando, segurando, surpreso, mostrando. Each component's parameters are in the examples edicoes/2026-10-28-mapa/roteiro.json and edicoes/2026-10-28-5-coisas/roteiro.json.
